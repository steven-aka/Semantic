# SEM-A0B query-conditioned grounded positive control

SEM-A0B evaluates only the generator contract `(question, one frozen rank10 source atom) -> <=16-token grounded fact`. It does not evaluate the whole semantic-compression idea. Inputs were selected by SHA from the 235 M0B-eligible canonical train queries outside M0C, M1, M2B, OBS-A1, and FRAG-B0. The generator loaded no gold answers, Target outputs, fidelity values, or action outcomes. Qwen3-14B ran greedily at batch size one; the emitted `source_quote` counts toward teacher output cost even though only `fact` would reach the Target.

The frozen first audit produced exact source quotes in 31/32 cases but only 18/32 facts within 16 Qwen3-8B tokens; only **8/32** passed all automatic necessary checks. Facts sometimes answered that a queried entity was “not mentioned,” or asserted a query-suggested relation not supported by the quote. Total teacher cost was 4,430 formatted prompt tokens and 1,365 generated tokens, with mean measured batch-1 generation latency 1.97 seconds (model loading excluded). These failures occur before the required independent predicate-level review.

Per the frozen rule, this cohort was permanently design-exposed. A revised prompt was tested on the next 32 SHA-ordered queries, not rescored on the first set. It explicitly forbade absence statements and constrained the fact to ten words as well as 16 tokens. This improved token compliance to 30/32 and mean fact length to 11.09 tokens, but exact quotes fell to 26/32 and only **18/32** passed all automatic checks. It still produced empty outputs, non-exact paraphrased quotes, and relations or queried entities unsupported by the selected atom. Teacher cost was 5,771 prompt tokens and 1,302 generated tokens, with mean batch-1 latency 1.90 seconds. Peak VRAM was not instrumented as an artifact, so the full cost contract is also incomplete.

The pre-registered manual gate required at least 31/32 facts with every predicate source-supported. Because the necessary automatic checks cap possible success far below 31, neither cohort proceeds to manual pass certification or Target calls. Decision: **`STOP_SEM_A0B_SINGLE_ATOM_GENERATOR_CONTRACT`**. No SEM-A1 or SEM-A2 is authorized.

This result does not show that semantic representation is ineffective. It shows that a Qwen3-14B prompt seeing only `(q, one future atom)` does not reliably produce the required short grounded action. A further hypothesis must change the contract rather than tune this audit: for example, permit explicit abstention and condition on current state to request only *novel* supported information. That would be a new SEM contract with a new untouched audit cohort and a separately frozen cost/coverage gate.
